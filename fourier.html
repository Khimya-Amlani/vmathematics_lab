    What does a coefficient actually measure?

    Every aₙ and bₙ answers one question: how much does f look like this test wave? Multiply f by the wave and add up the area under the product. Green area counts for, red area counts against. Pick n and press Scan.

    Every term is a spinning circle

    Harmonic n is a circle of radius √(aₙ² + bₙ²) that spins n times faster than the first. Stack the circles tip to tail: the last tip traces the partial sum. Tap a harmonic below to switch it off; the graph, the circles and the sound all lose it.

    Squeeze it: the uncertainty principle

    Stretch or squeeze f(x) and its spectrum does the opposite. A short pulse needs many frequencies to make its sudden start and stop; a long one needs only a few. Then listen: the pulse becomes the loudness envelope of a 660 Hz tone. Dashed curves show k = 1.
